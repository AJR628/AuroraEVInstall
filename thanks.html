Five things you can gather right now

If you have these ready when your electrician calls, they can give you a much more accurate ballpark on the first call instead of needing follow-up clarifications.

Quote prep checklist

5 minutes of prep saves a 30-minute clarification call later.

  • Take a photo of your main electrical panel with the door open (so we can see the breakers)
  • Note the panel's amperage — usually a number like 100A or 200A printed inside the door
  • Take a photo of the spot you want the charger mounted (the garage wall, exterior wall, etc.)
  • Walk the route from your panel to the charger and note any obstacles or finished surfaces
  • Know your EV make & model and (if you have a preference) which charger you're considering
